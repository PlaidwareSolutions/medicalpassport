What is left is not engineering:

- **Waiting on a person or a contract.** P9 clinical intelligence (OD-3 licensed
  catalog, OD-4 interaction provider, OD-6 clinical lead), the ABDM sandbox
  credentials for P8 (ticket 0.8), a lab partner for P13 levels 3–4, pilot

  (OD-11/OD-12).
- **Waiting on review.** Gate 4 clinical validation of the new copy, Gate 1b
  terminology review, and native review of roughly 900 draft hi/te/ur strings.
  H-19 governs the last of these: a translation that changes clinical meaning
  is a safety defect, so no locale ships on a machine translation.
- **Waiting on platform configuration.** The P0 tickets listed in the row above
  and the production `checkSuites` gate. The full leftover list, with how to
  do each item, is [22](22-handover-leftovers.md).

Two deliberate non-implementations to keep in view, both safety gates rather
than gaps: `unusual_measurement` caregiver alerts have no Safety-Board-approved
rule (H-41), and no screen anywhere computes an interpretation of a
patient-entered value (H-02, H-35, H-39, H-40, H-43).

### Playwright on this machine

The a11y and e2e sweeps were re-run per workstream as each screen set landed;
the full-suite numbers above are from the V2 baseline. The last full sweep read
633 passed / 6 failed, and all six are environment, not product: three Telugu
320 px overflows of exactly 7 px (Windows has only Nirmala UI; CI installs Noto)
and three timeouts on `/offline` and `/share-target` when several Next servers
were running at once. Two things to know before trusting a local run: it reuses
whatever dev server is already on :3000 unless you point it elsewhere, and
`--grep` cannot be trusted for a partial sweep — Playwright compiles the pattern
with the `g` flag and reuses one RegExp across titles, so `lastIndex` carries
over and silently skips tests. The authoritative result is Linux CI against a
production build.
