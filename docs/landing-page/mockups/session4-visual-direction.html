M2 · Problem story cardS2 Story B · corrected neutral wording · illustration style sample

Two names, one ingredient

Doctor A prescribes one brand. Doctor B prescribes another. Two different brand names may contain the same ingredient — and there's no easy way for a family to tell.

Illustration style sample: flat warm minimalism — ink line work, green + clay + sand fills only (illustration-only colors, decision 2), no faces here; the canonical set is commissioned in Session 8. The listen button is the site-wide opt-in narration pattern.

M4 · Free panelS10 · the page's only full-bleed color moment · wording GATED-BUSINESS/LEGAL

Medicine Passport is free for patients.

Your medicine information belongs to you. Medicine Passport is free for patients to create, maintain and share. We plan to sustain MediDocs through services and partnerships with healthcare organizations — not by charging patients for access to their own medicine information.

  • No advertising in the patient experience
  • Identifiable health information is never sold
  • No paywall on your own record

M5 · Trust sectionS11 · does / does-not · desktop 2-col

Built to help you understand your medicines — not to replace your doctor.

Medicine Passport does

  • Keep your medicine information organized — and yours.
  • Explain medicines in plain language where approved information is available.
  • Point out things worth asking your doctor about.
  • Let you share on your terms, and see every access.

Medicine Passport does not

  • It does not diagnose or prescribe.
  • It does not tell you to start or stop a medicine.
  • It does not substitute one medicine for another.
  • It does not declare any medicine "safe".
  • It does not replace doctors or pharmacists.

Meaning carried by wording and glyph, never color alone. Two of the "does" lines are gated (clinical / security) and drop from the list with clean seams if unapproved at launch.
